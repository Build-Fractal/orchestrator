### Scope Discipline

**What I removed.** I withdrew the new-command name (`orchestrator:note`/`:decide`) entirely — FR-7 now extends M040's already-on-disk `/orchestrator-capture` + `/orchestrator-promote` (Rec 5). I withdrew "live-read as optional configured augmentation" entirely — graduation is the sole guarantee-path mechanism, live-read deferred to M009 (Rec 4). I withdrew the "single-file change" justification for DQ-6 (Rec 3). These were my two genuine over-reaches: I was minting framework surface (a command) and coupling dispatch to a runtime shape (live-read), both of which the framework-integrity perspective correctly caught as integration debt.

**What new scope I still ask for.** Exactly one substantive scope item beyond proven bugs rides P0: the **explicit-decision-capture-at-Quick primitive** (Rec 2) — a one-line `append-decision.sh` call at Quick, plus the FR-6 compact-digest inject. Everything else I asked to promote (the capture *command* UX, auto-graduate, the two-store *graduation* build) I concede to the M040-track. The capture *command* does NOT ride P0; only the minimum write-side primitive M040 will later wrap.

**What remains fixed.** The §7 P0/P1 cleave-axis is correct and unchanged (Safe-Agreement with both reviewers). The proven-bug set (B-1..B-5) is P0. `.orchestrator/` is the system of record via graduation. Grep is the deterministic floor.

**Hold-or-concede — must BUG A (format mismatch) be P0?** **Hold.** Both reviewers agree it is a file:line-verified, source-of-truth-grade proven bug; framework-steward has "zero daylight" on it being P0. This was never actually in dispute — only its *foregrounding*, which both reviewers grant costs zero blast radius. BUG A stays P0, named first.

**Hold-or-concede — must capture-by-default be P0, or can it fold into M040 provided M040 ships soon?** **Partial concede, partial hold — and the conditional is the crux.** I concede the capture *command*, auto-graduate, and two-store *graduation build* fold into M040. I **hold** that the *explicit-decision-capture-at-Quick primitive* must ride P0, and I hold it *precisely because of the "provided M040 ships soon" conditional*. My cross-review of framework-steward (`[§0 provenance satisfies M040 trigger #2 and #4]`) verified against M040's own gate: §0 meets trigger #2 (one new consumer) but **not** #4 (requires ≥5 inbox friction reports; §0 has one field note). With only one of two trigger conditions cleanly met, **M040's formal trigger has NOT fired**, so "fold into M040 provided it ships soon" defers the capture primitive *indefinitely* — there is no committed date. The P0 hotfix is therefore the only path that ships a default capture loop on a committed timeline. If M040 had a committed near-term slot I would fold the whole loop; because it does not, the minimum write-side primitive must ride the hotfix so that FR-6's Quick Decisions slot is not shipped empty-forever (the subtler fail-open of Rec-2/`[G-1 home]`).

### Position Summary

Of my ten original recommendations: **five surviving** (Rec 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, 10 — six, counting Rec 10 which strengthened), **four modified** (Rec 3, 4, 5, 9), **zero fully withdrawn as recommendations** (though Rec 4 and Rec 5 each *withdrew a specific clause* — live-read and the new-command name — while keeping their core). Plus **two new recommendations** triggered by pipeline-reliability's two strongest contradictions (alarm-first sequencing; merged coverage+byte-stability AC). The deliberation converged hard: every "tension" resolved to composition, and the only two true contradictions raised against me (live-read coupling, alarm-vs-capture sequencing) I conceded cleanly without losing my central position.

The most significant change is **Rec 5 / Rec 4 taken together: I stop minting framework surface.** framework-steward's strongest challenge — that my `orchestrator:note` duplicates M040's already-specified `/orchestrator-capture`, and that my live-read couples dispatch to a runtime-specific shape — is correct on both counts, verified against on-disk M040 and against Principle VI. I withdraw both. My substance survives intact (capture is a distinct verb owning classification + confirmation; `.orchestrator/` is the system of record), but it now rides *existing* framework primitives rather than new ones. This is the honest correction: my round-trip diagnosis was right, but my proposed *vehicles* were over-built, and the framework-integrity perspective caught the latent integration debt before it shipped.

My remaining highest-priority recommendation is **Rec 2 (modified): ship the explicit-decision-capture-at-Quick primitive in P0, in the same change set as FR-6's compact bounded Decisions digest.** It is the one scope item I refuse to concede, and the reason is now load-bearing-verified rather than asserted: M040's formal trigger has not fired (only #2, not #4), so deferring the capture primitive to M040 defers it with no committed date, and FR-6 in P0 without it ships a Quick Decisions slot that is empty forever — a fail-open subtler than the original bug because the surface reports healthy. The capture *command* and the graduation *build* defer; the minimum write-side primitive does not. That single one-line `append-decision.sh`-at-Quick call, sequenced *after* the FR-15 alarm (New-1), is what makes the default project's happy path actually exist rather than merely be parseable.
