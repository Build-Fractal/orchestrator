### Dangerous Contradictions

- **[net-new-capture-command-vs-M040-already-specified]** — activation-loop claims (Rec 5, `[specify-FR-7-capture-command]`) that FR-7 should resolve toward "a **new first-class command** (`orchestrator:note` with `--decision`/`--knowledge` modes) rather than a `:do` flag — `:do` is task-dispatch-shaped; capture is a distinct verb and deserves discoverability." framework-steward claims (my review, Missed-Opp `[FR-7 should BE M040's .../orchestrator-capture]` + Rec 4 `[fold-capture-into-M040]`) that M040 *already names* `/orchestrator-capture` (raw landing) + `/orchestrator-promote --to=mem|decision|task` (explicit graduation) as Surface 3, with storage paths and frontmatter schema already specified. **Why dangerous (roadmap-coherence / Single-Source-of-Truth failure):** activation-loop and I *agree* a capture verb is distinct from `:do` and deserves its own surface — but if the deliberation adopts activation-loop's `orchestrator:note`/`:decide` naming, the framework ships a capture command that M040 must then reconcile or deprecate, producing two parallel capture loops the very moment M040 enters the queue. I independently verified M040 §"Three surfaces" / §"Lifecycle commands" (`/orchestrator-capture`, `/orchestrator-promote`, `/orchestrator-inbox`, `/orchestrator-promote --to=decision`) — the verb activation-loop wants minting already exists by name on disk. **Cooperative resolution:** keep activation-loop's *substance* (capture is its own verb, owns classification + confirmation, zero GitHub dependency — all correct and valuable) but bind it to M040's *existing* names: FR-7 becomes "M040's `/orchestrator-capture` + `/orchestrator-promote`, extended with activation-loop's round-trip-confirmation requirement and local-classification ownership." We both get the discoverable distinct verb; the framework gets one loop, not two.

- **[read-runtime-memory-directly-vs-runtime-agnostic-posture]** — activation-loop, in Rec 4 (`[resolve-DQ-8-system-of-record-with-enforcement]`), endorses graduation (b) as default *with* live-read (a) as "an optional configured augmentation" — but DQ-8's live-read branch is "`build-context.sh` ... reads the runtime's agent memory (configurable path)." framework-steward claims (Rec 6 `[place-FR-9 as M040 precondition]`) that live runtime-memory read couples dispatch to a runtime-specific memory *shape* (CC's `MEMORY.md` format), violating the launch-posture runtime-agnostic contract and Principle VI (State On Disk Is Truth — `.orchestrator/` is truth, not a per-runtime store). **Why dangerous (runtime-parity regression):** even as an *optional* augmentation, a shipped live-read path establishes a precedent that dispatch can depend on a runtime's memory format. The moment a non-CC user arrives (M009's trigger), that path either breaks or forces a per-runtime adapter the framework explicitly deferred. The coupling is latent debt that fires exactly when runtime expansion begins. **Cooperative resolution:** we converge on graduation-as-default (activation-loop says it, I say it). Lock live-read *out of scope entirely* — not "optional configured augmentation" but "explicitly deferred to M009-era runtime-memory adapter work." Graduation keeps `.orchestrator/` self-contained; the divergence-warning (FR-15) makes silent drift loud without coupling. activation-loop's enforcement clause ("silent divergence not possible by default") is fully satisfiable by graduation + warning alone — no live-read needed.

- **[promote-capture-loop-to-P0-vs-blast-radius-discipline]** — activation-loop claims (Rec 1/2/3 + Missed-Opp `[demand-signal-already-fired]`) that G-1/FR-8 (Quick-default capture) and the round-trip capture core belong in **P0** alongside the proven-bug fixes, because "the demand signal has already fired" and "half the loop is hotfixed; the other half is deferred." framework-steward claims (Rec 1 `[cleave-physical-split]` + Off-Base `[T4/T5 are net-new Activation Build scope]`) that the P0 hotfix must stay tightly bounded to the *proven-bug pipeline* (FR-1,2,3,4,5,11,15) and that the capture loop is M040's already-specified surface — promote M040, don't grow the hotfix. **Why dangerous (P0 scope-creep / ship-date hostage):** if the capture loop joins P0, the hotfix's ship date becomes hostage to the capture-command design fork (DQ-7), the system-of-record fork (DQ-8), and the Quick-token-budget question (FR-6/Principle I) — exactly the deliberation-heavy questions a trust-recovery hotfix should not wait on. The proven bugs are file:line-cited and ready; the capture loop is design work. Bundling delays the bugs. **Cooperative resolution:** this is partly a *tension* (see below) not a pure contradiction — we agree the demand has fired. Split the difference along the actual fault line: the *explicit-decision-capture-at-Quick* half of FR-8 (G-1) is small, mechanical ("always run `append-decision.sh` for explicit decisions even at Quick"), and arguably proven-gap-grade — it can ride P0. The *new capture command* (FR-7), *auto-graduate-from-execution-log* (FR-8 second half), and *two-store bridge* (FR-9) are M040-shaped design and promote M040 instead. This gives activation-loop the "default project actually captures" win without making the hotfix carry a command-design fork.

### Tensions

- **[capture-by-default-at-Quick vs Context-Minimization]** — activation-loop's position: Quick must *always* capture explicit decisions and the Quick inject must carry Decisions (FR-6/FR-8 to P0), because "the most dangerous place to forget a decision is a Quick change, which today gets the least context." framework-steward's position: Principle I (v2.3.0, "minimize *total* task tokens") and the M031 right-sizing work mean Quick is deliberately slim — adding Decisions unconditionally risks re-inflating the profile the framework just slimmed; phrase FR-6 as "compact decisions *digest* bounded by the Quick token budget." **Nature of tension:** both are correct and pull opposite ways — activation-loop optimizes *recall* (never lose a decision), I optimize *cost* (never re-inflate Quick). This is the productive under-load-vs-over-load axis. **Coordination needed:** agree the digest is *bounded* (my budget concern) AND *always present* (activation-loop's recall concern) — a compact bounded digest satisfies both. Route the digest through M036a's token-budget governor so "always capture/inject" never becomes "ungoverned firehose."

- **[ship-capture-loop-NOW vs demand-driven-roadmap-discipline]** — activation-loop's position (`[demand-signal-already-fired]`, `[P1-is-demand-driven]` Off-Base): the field note proves demand has fired; treating the loop as "demand-driven" mis-models an active trust-erosion bug as a feature-want. framework-steward's position (Rec 5 `[promote-M040 / annotate demand signal]`): I *agree* demand has fired — M040's §Trigger conditions #2 and #4 are now met by this proposal's dual-source provenance — but the correct response to "demand fired" is *promote M040 into the queue*, not *build M040's surfaces under a hotfix label*. **Nature of tension:** we agree on the signal; we differ on the mechanism. activation-loop reads "demand fired" → "ship now in P0." I read "demand fired" → "promote the milestone that already specifies this." **Coordination needed:** both moves are compatible — annotate M040's brief with the fired demand signal (my Rec 5) *and* let the small mechanical G-1 slice ride P0 (activation-loop's Rec 2). The capture *command* design lands in a promoted M040, not an expanded hotfix.

- **[foreground-BUG-A-as-co-primary vs intake-bounding]** — activation-loop's position (Rec 1 `[promote-BUG-A-to-P0-co-primary]`): BUG A (B-3, format mismatch) is a *source-of-truth* failure (the canonical store is unreadable), strictly more fundamental than the rebuild *cache* failure it sits beside, and must be foregrounded as co-primary in P0. framework-steward's position: I agree BUG A is in P0 and agree on the cache-vs-source-of-truth distinction (my P-B/Principle-XI framing draws the same line) — my concern is only that the *intake stay bounded* to proven bugs, not that BUG A be de-emphasized. **Nature of tension:** near-zero — this is emphasis alignment, not conflict. activation-loop wants BUG A *louder*; I want the P0 set *smaller*. Both are served by a P0 that is FR-1,2,3,4,5,11,15 with FR-1 (BUG A) named first. **Coordination needed:** adopt activation-loop's "B-1 is a recoverable cache failure (P-B-tolerable); BUG A is an unreadable-source-of-truth failure" framing verbatim as the P0 ordering rationale — it strengthens my bounding argument rather than competing with it.

- **[grep-floor-for-round-trip-immediacy vs determinism-as-invariant]** — activation-loop's position (Rec 10 `[keep-grep-fallback-deterministic-for-round-trip]`): endorse the grep floor because the round-trip's *confirmation step* needs deterministic, index-free, same-instant retrieval — embeddings add latency/nondeterminism between capture and resolution. framework-steward's position (Rec 3 `[defend-determinism]`): resolve DQ-2 as a stated *invariant* — deterministic grep is the activation guarantee and the only input to the corpus-gate evidence artifact; embeddings, if ever added, are additive recall that never gates and never enters the receipt. **Nature of tension:** complementary, not conflicting — activation-loop argues grep-floor from the *round-trip-immediacy* angle, I argue it from the *reproducible-trust-receipt* (Principle IX) angle. Same conclusion, two independent justifications. **Coordination needed:** combine both rationales into the DQ-2 resolution so it's load-bearing from two directions (immediacy + reproducibility) — a stronger lock than either alone.

- **[auto-graduate qualification rule — phase-close vs continuous]** — activation-loop's position (Rec 8 `[specify-auto-graduate-qualification]`): graduate `decision:`-tagged notes at *phase close* (consolidate boundary), not continuously, to keep the system-of-record store clean. framework-steward's position: I flagged the same risk (auto-graduate could pollute the authoritative store and degrade every inject — Principle I/VI) but did not specify the trigger. **Nature of tension:** none on direction — activation-loop supplies the precise trigger my review only gestured at. **Coordination needed:** adopt activation-loop's phase-close-`decision:`-tagged rule as the FR-8 graduation trigger; it's the missing mechanism that makes my "keep `.orchestrator/` clean" concern enforceable.
