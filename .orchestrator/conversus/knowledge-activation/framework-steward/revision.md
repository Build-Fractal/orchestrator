## Position Summary

Of my ten original recommendations: **3 Surviving** (Rec 3 defend-determinism, Rec 9 FR-12 advisory-gating, Rec 10 keep-DQ-1-out-of-P0), **7 Modified** (Recs 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), **0 Withdrawn**. I add **3 New Recommendations** (G-1 capture slice to P0, bounded unguarded-pipeline audit artifact, byte-equality fixtures sequenced to schema-settledness). No recommendation collapsed to nothing — but the central frame shifted materially.

The most significant change is the **decoupling of command-name from ship-timing** on the capture loop. Both cross-reviews delivered the same load-bearing challenge from different lanes: my "fold into M040" silently read as "defer until M040 is scheduled," and because M040's trigger is demand-driven *and* — as both agents proved factually — not cleanly fired (#4 needs ≥5 reports, §0 supplies one), that deferral would have been indefinite. I conceded that BUG A (B-3 format mismatch) is a proven P0 bug (I never disputed this, but I now *foreground* it as co-primary using activation-loop's cache-vs-source-of-truth framing), that the FR-5 grep-fallback retrieval belongs in P0 (budgeted), and that the G-1 explicit-decision-capture slice must ride P0 as the local primitive M040 later wraps. The framework gets one capture command name and zero parallel loops — but it gets the *minimum mechanism* now, not on a queue that may never move.

My remaining highest-priority recommendation is **New Recommendation A: ship the G-1 capture slice in P0**, atomically with FR-6's inject half. It is the direct answer to the challenge both agents pressed: it closes the silent-store hole on every new Quick project on a committed timeline, it costs zero blast radius (a row-append, not a retrieval engine or a new command), and it preserves the roadmap discipline I came in defending — M040 still owns the capture *verb* and *UX*; it just no longer gates the *existence* of a default capture path. The constitution lines I continue to hold — token-governor on the grep firehose, live-read out of scope, deterministic grep floor — are the three places where conceding would trade a proven fix for a new silent failure, and those do not bend.
