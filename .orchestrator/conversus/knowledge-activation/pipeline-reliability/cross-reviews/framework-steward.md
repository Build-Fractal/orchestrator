### Dangerous Contradictions

- **[fail-loud-observability-is-P0-vs-deferred-to-M040]** — framework-steward (Missed-Opportunities #1, Actionable Rec 1 `[cleave-physical-split]`) wants FR-15 *kept* in P0 but routes the broader observability surface (0-MEM/degraded/freshness signalling) toward the P1/M040 track, treating "observability everywhere" as activation-build feature scope. pipeline-reliability holds (my review, §Exec-Summary; Recs 8, 10, 11) that **fail-loud observability IS the P0 reliability core, not a feature** — its *absence* is the single root cause that let `rebuild-index.sh:117` freeze the index silently for ~1 month across two milestones with nobody told. **Why dangerous (failure mode: silent-degradation-recurs-during-the-fix-window):** if the loud-on-degradation surface is split off into a demand-driven M040 track, the P0 hotfix can ship a *corrected* pipeline that still has no alarm when it next degrades — re-creating the exact "green but empty, no signal" condition (my §2 G-6; B-2 at `build-context.sh:208`, confirmed: silent `head -5` fallback under `|| true`, zero warning). A pipeline fixed-but-unobservable is one regression away from the original month-long outage. **Cooperative resolution:** agree FR-15's *0-MEM/degraded-source warning + provenance header emission* (the alarm) ships in P0 alongside the bug fixes; defer only the *richer ambient surfacing* (brief integration, inbox composition) to M040. The alarm is the cheapest insurance against the fix silently rotting; the ambient loop is genuinely M040. This splits framework-steward's "observability is M040 feature" cleanly from my "the alarm is the hotfix."

- **[FR-5-minimum-cut-strips-the-load-bearing-P-B-guarantee]** — framework-steward (Rec 2 `[bound-P0-hotfix]`) proposes admitting FR-5 "only at its *minimum* (emit a visible WARNING + provenance flag)" and **deferring the grep-over-raw-files retrieval to P1/M040** as "a feature, not a bug fix." pipeline-reliability holds (my Rec 11, Rec 1, Rec 5) that the **index-free grep-over-raw-files fallback IS the P-B guarantee** and is itself a proven-bug surface (G-6: index green, scope-unmatchable, re-triggers first-N → a *non-zero but degraded* inject), so at least the "fallback returns hits with index AND db both deleted" assertion must land in P0. **Why dangerous (failure mode: warn-loudly-then-inject-nothing):** a P0 that warns "degraded" but has no raw-file retrieval path produces a *loud* failure that still ships an empty/first-N payload — fail-loud without fail-*over*. The agent is correctly told it is under-informed and then proceeds under-informed anyway, which on a Quick path (Decisions already omitted, confirmed `build-context.sh:223`) is exactly the confident-wrong-work condition P-A exists to prevent. **Cooperative resolution:** keep framework-steward's token-governor concern (legitimate — see Tensions) but pull the *minimal* index-free path into P0: grep raw `knowledge/**/*.md` on degraded/empty index, bounded by the existing M036a governor. The warning and the fallback are one mechanism, not a P0 half and a P1 half.

That satisfies steward's blast-radius bound and my enumeration demand simultaneously.

The 0-MEM/degraded warning + provenance header = P0 (standalone). The brief/inbox/contradiction-gate composition of that signal = M040. Neither of us loses our load-bearing concern.
