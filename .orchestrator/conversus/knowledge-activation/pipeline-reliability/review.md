## Executive Summary

The consolidated proposal is a faithful and largely complete uptake of the reliability thesis my project submitted. It correctly elevates my two load-bearing principles to named, first-class invariants — **P-A (silent degradation is the enemy; fail loud, never fail open or closed-silent)** and **P-B (the index is a cache, the raw corpus is truth, the guarantee runs index-free)** — and it preserves them verbatim from my submission (`upstream-proposal, L13-21`; `handoff, L6`). Every proven bug I root-caused via `bash -x` survives into the evidence table with live file:line citations (B-1 = `rebuild-index.sh:117`, B-2 = `build-context.sh:198-208`), and the proposal correctly merges Source 2's capture→store→inject findings into a third principle (P-C) without diluting mine. The phasing split (§7) is exactly right from a reliability standpoint: the proven, actively-burning bugs are carved into a milestone-independent P0 hotfix, and the larger activation-loop build folds into M040 as P1. That is the correct blast-radius posture — trust recovery should not wait on a milestone queue.

Where the proposal serves my domain best is in keeping the **index-free grep guarantee** as the architectural spine (FR-5, FR-12, FR-14, DQ-1, DQ-2) and in mandating the **provenance header** as a payload-resident, agent-visible artifact rather than a stderr-only courtesy (FR-5c). Where it serves my domain *least* is in granularity: the audit of *other* unguarded `set -e`/`pipefail` commands is named but not scoped to a concrete deliverable; the index-free fallback is mandated for `build-context.sh` but only gestured at for "every index reader" without an enumerated consumer list; the provenance-header schema is sketched but not pinned to a byte-stable contract; and the regression tests (AC §5) are specified behaviorally rather than at the fixture-byte-equality granularity my project's hard-won lesson demands. None of these are wrong — they are under-specified, and under-specification is exactly how the original `:117` bug shipped (a guard that "should" be there, never tested for).

**Most important recommendation:** make the index-free deterministic-grep fallback a *mandated, enumerated, fixture-byte-equality-tested* invariant for **every** index consumer — not just `build-context.sh` — because a fail-loud guarantee that only covers one of N readers is itself a silent fail-open for the other N-1.

## Alignment

- **[fail-loud thesis preserved verbatim]** (§1 P-A): The proposal lifts my "fail loud, never fail open or closed-silent" framing word-for-word and makes it the organizing principle of the whole FR set. This is the exact distinction I drew — an agent that *knows* it is under-informed behaves correctly; one silently under-informed produces confident wrong work `[upstream-proposal, L13-15]`, `[handoff, L6, L58]`.

- **[index-as-cache architecturally load-bearing]** (§1 P-B, FR-10, FR-11, DQ-1): The proposal correctly states the index "makes activation *fast*; it must never be what makes activation *possible*" and demotes it to a cache behind a freshness contract, with a single canonical resolver. This is my P-B `[upstream-proposal, L18-21]` and the canonicalization ask `[handoff, L73-74, FR-6]`.

- **[resilience over atomicity in the rebuild]** (§3 T2 / FR-3): Per-entry try/skip/warn with `INDEXED: N / SKIPPED: M [ids/paths]` and exit-non-zero-only-on-catastrophic is exactly my "one malformed entry must degrade *that entry*, never the whole operation" `[upstream-proposal, L62-65]`, `[handoff, L54-55]`. The proposal also keeps my explicit demand to "audit the whole script for every other unguarded command under `set -e`/`pipefail`" (FR-3 body) — credited, even if not yet scoped (see Missed Opportunities).

- **[provenance travels with context, in the payload]** (§3 T3 / FR-5c): The `source: index|grep-fallback|degraded`, `index_age`, `entries_considered` header is mandated *into the injected payload* and stderr, not stderr-only. This is my fourth principle — "provenance travels with context" — landed correctly `[upstream-proposal, L66-69]`, `[handoff, L58, FR-2]`.

- **[corpus-gate is the index-independent guarantee]** (§3 T7 / FR-12, §6): The proposal treats `corpus-gate` as the resilient, index-independent grep primitive and defaults it onto the highest-damage paths, while explicitly calling out "the gate's index-independence is the load-bearing asset; verify and preserve it" (§6). That is precisely my framing — "the index is an optimization; the gate is the guarantee" `[handoff, L68]`.

- **[the subtler fail-open is captured]** (§2 G-6, FR-14): The proposal preserves my "index present ≠ index useful" finding — REF chunks indexing with empty `scope_tags` + bare-ID descriptions report green but are unqueryable — and mandates degrade-to-grep so quality is "not hostage to hand-authored tags." This is my scope-matching caveat, intact `[upstream-proposal, L114-143]`, `[handoff, L33-34, L70-71]`.

## Missed Opportunities

- **[unguarded-command audit is named, not scoped]** — FR-3 repeats my "audit the whole script for every other unguarded command under `set -e`/`pipefail`" but produces no concrete deliverable (no enumerated list, no acceptance criterion that the audit ran). My stance: the audit must yield a *checked-in artifact* — a list of every pipeline in `rebuild-index.sh` (and its sourced libs `lib/index-utils.sh`, `lib/graph-db.sh`) classified guarded/unguarded, with the unguarded ones either fixed or justified. The `:117` bug existed precisely because the guard was assumed, never enumerated; `fm_field` at `:40` ends in `|| true`, `:117` does not, and nothing tested the difference. Concrete benefit: converts "we audited" (unverifiable claim) into a reproducible artifact a reviewer can diff. `[upstream-proposal, L73-77]`, `[handoff, L55]`. **Impact: high.**

- **[index-free fallback mandated for one consumer, gestured for the rest]** — FR-5 says "`build-context.sh` and every index reader," but only `build-context.sh:198-208` is cited and only its fallback is specified. "Every index reader" is not enumerated. My stance: produce the canonical consumer list (grep for readers of `KNOWLEDGE-INDEX.md` / `knowledge.db` / `resolve-entries.sh`) and mandate the index-free path + provenance header for *each*. A fail-loud guarantee covering 1 of N readers is a silent fail-open for the other N-1 — the same disease, relocated. Concrete benefit: closes the "we fixed the one we found" trap that let this class hide for a month. `[upstream-proposal, L74-77, FR-5]`, `[handoff, L57-58, FR-2]`. **Impact: high.**

- **[provenance header has no byte-stable schema]** — FR-5c names three fields but pins neither their key names, ordering, value enums, nor the line shape. My project's hard lesson is that injected-payload contracts must be byte-stable or downstream parsers drift. Stance: define the header as an exact, versioned line (e.g. fixed key order, fixed `source:` enum `{index,grep-fallback,degraded}`, an explicit `schema_version`) and lock it with a fixture. Concrete benefit: a second consumer (M034 decision-packets, M038 living-docs, the wiki surface) can parse provenance deterministically instead of regex-sniffing. `[upstream-proposal, L66-69]`, `[handoff, L58]`. **Impact: medium.**

- **[regression tests specified behaviorally, not at fixture-byte-equality]** — AC §5 items 2/4/7 describe *behaviors* ("rebuilds successfully," "visibly states it ran degraded," "emits a visible warning") but not *expected bytes*. My domain's default is byte-equality fixtures: `expected == actual` over a committed corpus, because substring/"contains a warning" asserts are asymptotic-not-convergent — they pass while drifting. Stance: each regression (heading-less rebuild summary line, degraded-payload header, freshness flag) ships a committed fixture corpus + an exact expected-output file, asserted byte-for-byte. Concrete benefit: the `INDEXED: N / SKIPPED: M` summary and the provenance header become regression-locked, not vibe-checked. `[handoff, L76-79, FR-7]`. **Impact: high.**

- **[freshness contract under-specifies the staleness signal]** — FR-10 says "compare index mtime/content-hash against the newest `knowledge/**/*.md`" but leaves mtime-vs-hash as an or. mtime is unreliable across `orchestrator:update` re-installs and git checkouts (which rewrite mtimes wholesale); content-hash is the durable signal. Stance: make content-hash the contract and mtime at most a fast pre-filter. Concrete benefit: avoids false-green freshness after an `update` touches files without changing content, and false-stale after a checkout. `[handoff, L60-61, FR-3]`. **Impact: medium.**

- **[the vestigial-path trap is folded into FR-11 but loses its "misled a careful investigator" weight]** — FR-11 canonicalizes the path but the proposal drops my finding that the duplicate `.orchestrator/KNOWLEDGE-INDEX.md` / `.orchestrator/knowledge.db` actively misdirected diagnosis. Stance: FR-11 should not just "remove/alias" — it should add a `doctor` check that *asserts no second index/db exists* on disk, because a silently-present vestigial copy is itself a fail-open (an investigator fixes the wrong file and "verifies" against a stale one). Concrete benefit: turns a one-time cleanup into a standing invariant. `[upstream-proposal, L86-87]`, `[handoff, L30-31, L73-74]`. **Impact: medium.**

- **[0-MEM warning threshold is binary; staleness/thin-metadata green-but-empty is not covered by the same alarm]** — FR-15 warns on a 0-MEM inject on a project with prior milestones. But G-6's subtler fail-open (index green, scope-unmatchable, re-triggers first-N) can yield a *non-zero* inject that is still degraded. Stance: the doctor/observability check should warn not only on 0 MEMs but on `source: grep-fallback|degraded` appearing in an inject on a mature project, and on "REF chunks indexed with empty `scope_tags`." Concrete benefit: catches the green-but-empty case that a pure 0-MEM gate misses. `[upstream-proposal, L57, G-6, L114-131]`, `[handoff, L33-34]`. **Impact: medium.**

- **[corpus-gate evidence-artifact reproducibility is asserted, not pinned to determinism guarantees]** — AC §9 / FR-12 require the evidence artifact be "reproducible (same inputs → same log)" but do not forbid the nondeterminism vectors that break reproducibility (locale-dependent `sort`, timestamp lines, filesystem-glob ordering). Stance: mandate `LC_ALL=C` sort, no wall-clock fields in the artifact body, and stable file ordering — the same disciplines that make the grep trust-receipt actually trustworthy. Concrete benefit: the "deterministic trust receipt" is real, not aspirational. `[upstream-proposal, L93, L105]`, `[handoff, L67]`. **Impact: medium.**

- **[no explicit guarantee that the grep fallback runs against RAW files, not re-derived from the index]** — FR-5b says "grep-over-raw-files fallback" but the spec never forbids a lazy implementation that greps a cached/serialized form. The whole point of P-B is that the *raw corpus* is the source of truth. Stance: state that the fallback's input set is the on-disk `knowledge/**/*.md` files directly, resolved independently of the index/db, and test that the fallback still returns hits when the index *and* db are both deleted. Concrete benefit: prevents a "fallback" that secretly depends on the thing it is meant to survive. `[upstream-proposal, L18-21, L28]`, `[handoff, L57-58, L68]`. **Impact: high.**

## Off-Base Assumptions

- **[B-3 column-mismatch line numbers are slightly off / self-inconsistent against live source]** (§2 table, FR-1, DQ-6): The proposal says `filter_decisions` "reads Scope from `$4`, When from `$5`." Live source at `scripts/dispatch/scope-filter.sh:351-354` *comments* `1=ID, 2=Decision, 3=Choice, 4=Scope, 5=When, 6=Rationale` but the actual awk reads `scope_col` from `$5` and `when_col` from `$6` (because `awk -F'|'` on a leading-pipe markdown row makes `$1` empty, shifting every column by one). The *defect is real and worse than stated* — the consumer's own inline comment disagrees with its own awk indices — but the proposal's "$4/$5" claim doesn't match either the comment or the code. Correct understanding: the round-trip test (FR-1) must be authored against the *observed awk field indices*, not the documented column order, and DQ-6 must reconcile three things, not two (producer format, consumer comment, consumer awk). This strengthens rather than weakens the FR — but the citation should be corrected so the fix targets the right field index. `[live: scope-filter.sh:351-354]`, ref `[upstream-proposal, L44, L109]`. (Note: this is in Source 2's lane more than mine; flagged because it affects the round-trip oracle's correctness.)

- **[Otherwise: no off-base reliability assumptions found.]** The two defects in my direct lane — B-1 (`rebuild-index.sh:117` unguarded grep under `set -euo pipefail`, with guarded sibling at `:40`) and B-2 (`build-context.sh:208` silent first-5 fallback, no warning, Decisions section omitted at `:223`) — are confirmed accurate against live source as cited. The archive-glob finding (B-4) is also consistent: the script's own header comment (`rebuild-index.sh:6`) declares the intended exclusion is `knowledge/archive/`, which confirms the bare `*/archive/*` form is a defect, not the design.

## Actionable Recommendations

1. **[mandate-index-free-fallback-for-all-consumers]** (Priority P0) — *Current state:* FR-5 enumerates only `build-context.sh`. *Proposed change:* produce a checked-in consumer inventory (every reader of `KNOWLEDGE-INDEX.md`, `knowledge.db`, `resolve-entries.sh`) and require the deterministic-grep-over-raw-files fallback + provenance header for each; add a regression that deletes BOTH index and db and asserts every consumer still returns scoped hits. *Scope boundary:* P0 hotfix; no new retrieval semantics, only fallback coverage. *Rationale:* a fail-loud guarantee covering 1 of N readers is a silent fail-open for the rest — the same disease relocated `[upstream-proposal, L18-21, L74-77]`, `[handoff, L57-58]`. *Risk if ignored:* the next consumer reintroduces the exact `build-context.sh` failure mode, undetected.

2. **[scope-the-unguarded-command-audit-to-an-artifact]** (Priority P0) — *Current state:* FR-3 names the audit, defines no deliverable. *Proposed change:* emit a committed audit artifact enumerating every pipeline in `rebuild-index.sh` + sourced libs, classified guarded/unguarded, each unguarded one fixed or justified; add an AC that the audit artifact exists and lists zero unjustified-unguarded pipelines. *Scope boundary:* P0; `rebuild-index.sh` and its direct `source`d libs only. *Rationale:* `:117` shipped because the guard was assumed not enumerated; `:40` is guarded, `:117` is not, nothing tested the asymmetry `[upstream-proposal, L73-77]`, `[handoff, L55]`. *Risk if ignored:* a sibling unguarded pipeline reproduces the month-long silent freeze.

3. **[lock-regressions-at-fixture-byte-equality]** (Priority P0) — *Current state:* AC §5 (2/4/7) is behavioral. *Proposed change:* each P0 regression ships a committed fixture corpus + exact expected-output file, asserted byte-for-byte (the `INDEXED:/SKIPPED:` summary line, the degraded-payload provenance header block, the freshness-flag output). *Scope boundary:* test harness only; no runtime change. *Rationale:* substring/"contains a warning" asserts are asymptotic-not-convergent — they pass while drifting; byte-equality is the convergent default `[handoff, L76-79]`. *Risk if ignored:* the fail-loud surfaces silently drift and re-degrade without a failing test.

4. **[pin-the-provenance-header-as-a-versioned-byte-contract]** (Priority P1) — *Current state:* FR-5c names three fields, no schema. *Proposed change:* define an exact line/block with fixed key order, `source:` enum `{index,grep-fallback,degraded}`, `index_age`, `entries_considered`, and a `schema_version`; lock with a fixture; document it where M034/M038/wiki consumers can read it. *Scope boundary:* the header contract itself; consumers adopt incrementally. *Rationale:* injected-payload contracts must be byte-stable or downstream parsers drift; this is the composition point §6 promises to M034/M038 `[upstream-proposal, L66-69, L136-137]`, `[handoff, L58]`. *Risk if ignored:* every consumer regex-sniffs provenance, and the header becomes unmaintainable.

5. **[resolve DQ-1 — demote the index to cache, with an index-free path on EVERY consumer]** (Priority P0) — *Position:* demote (Sources' lean, and mine). *Current state:* DQ-1 open. *Proposed change:* adopt "cache, may be absent"; the gated-optimization alternative is rejected because a hard freshness gate that *blocks* dispatch on a stale index reintroduces a single point of failure for whether context is considered at all — the precise property P-B forbids. *Scope boundary:* architectural stance; implemented via Rec 1. *Rationale:* `[upstream-proposal, L18-21, L104]`, `[handoff, L68]`. *Risk if ignored:* the index stays load-bearing and the class is unfixed.

6. **[resolve DQ-2 — deterministic grep is the floor; embeddings only additive]** (Priority P1) — *Position:* grep-floor, embeddings-as-boost-only (my submitted lean). *Current state:* DQ-2 open. *Proposed change:* mandate substring/grep as the guarantee for the thin-metadata fallback; permit embeddings only as an additive recall booster that never replaces the deterministic floor and never enters the corpus-gate evidence artifact. *Scope boundary:* the *guarantee* is grep; embeddings live outside the trust receipt. *Rationale:* embeddings are nondeterministic, cost tokens, need a model, and break the reproducible evidence artifact that makes the gate auditable `[upstream-proposal, L105, L145-153 of upstream]`, `[handoff, L142-152]`. *Risk if ignored:* the trust receipt stops being reproducible (AC §9 fails), and the zero-LLM property that makes corpus-gate trustworthy is lost.

7. **[resolve DQ-3 — rebuild-then-warn-if-still-bad; never auto-rebuild silently mid-dispatch]** (Priority P1) — *Position:* rebuild-then-warn-if-still-bad. *Current state:* DQ-3 open. *Proposed change:* on detected staleness, attempt one rebuild, then re-check; if still stale/degraded, fail LOUD via the provenance header + stderr and proceed on the index-free path — but the rebuild must itself be the resilient per-entry version (FR-3), and a *silent* auto-rebuild (no provenance trace) is forbidden. *Scope boundary:* dispatch path; warn-only is the fallback if rebuild is unsafe in-context. *Rationale:* auto-rebuild adds latency + nondeterminism mid-dispatch, but warn-only relies on an operator who, per the evidence, was never told for a month; the hybrid keeps the loud-on-failure invariant `[upstream-proposal, L106]`, `[handoff, L60-61]`. *Risk if ignored:* either silent latency/nondeterminism, or a stale index nobody acts on — both fail-quiet.

8. **[resolve DQ-4 — header always + stderr always; hard gate only on highest-stakes paths]** (Priority P0) — *Position:* my submitted lean. *Current state:* DQ-4 open. *Proposed change:* provenance header into the payload *always*, stderr *always*; a dispatch-refusing hard gate reserved for explicitly-flagged highest-stakes paths only (not the default, to avoid trading fail-open for fail-closed-blocking). *Scope boundary:* loudness policy. *Rationale:* the agent receiving context must always be able to tell real scoped knowledge from a degraded fallback; a blanket hard gate would itself become a brittle single point of failure `[upstream-proposal, L107]`, `[handoff, L58, FR-2]`. *Risk if ignored:* either degradation stays invisible (status quo) or dispatch becomes globally brittle.

9. **[make-content-hash-the-freshness-contract]** (Priority P1) — *Current state:* FR-10 leaves mtime-or-hash ambiguous. *Proposed change:* content-hash of the newest `knowledge/**/*.md` set is the freshness signal; mtime is at most a fast pre-filter. *Scope boundary:* freshness check + doctor wiring. *Rationale:* `orchestrator:update` re-installs and git checkouts rewrite mtimes wholesale → mtime yields false-green/false-stale; the handoff itself warns the local `|| true` fix is non-durable because `update` overwrites it, the same mtime-churn surface `[handoff, L46, L60-61]`. *Risk if ignored:* freshness reports green after an update touches files without changing content.

10. **[assert-no-vestigial-index-as-a-standing-doctor-check]** (Priority P1) — *Current state:* FR-11 removes/aliases the duplicate path once. *Proposed change:* add an `orchestrator:doctor` invariant that asserts exactly one index + one db exist at the canonical location and fails loud if a second `.orchestrator/KNOWLEDGE-INDEX.md` / `.orchestrator/knowledge.db` reappears. *Scope boundary:* doctor check + FR-11. *Rationale:* the vestigial copy "misled even a careful investigator" — a silently-present second index is a fail-open that causes fixes/verifications against the wrong file `[upstream-proposal, L86-87]`, `[handoff, L30-31]`. *Risk if ignored:* the ambiguity recurs and re-misdirects the next diagnosis.

11. **[keep §7 P0/P1 phasing — ship proven bugs milestone-independent]** (Priority P0) — *Current state:* §7 already splits P0 hotfix from P1 M040 fold-in. *Proposed change:* keep unchanged; this is the correct reliability posture. Only adjustment: pull FR-14's index-free degrade-to-grep guarantee (currently P1) *partially* into P0 — specifically the "fallback runs against raw files even with index+db deleted" regression — since it is the load-bearing P-B assertion and is hotfix-shaped. *Scope boundary:* phasing only. *Rationale:* the proven bugs are eroding trust today; trust recovery should not wait on M040, and the index-free guarantee is the one P1 item that is itself a proven-bug surface (G-6) `[upstream-proposal, L142-148]`, `[handoff, L52]`. *Risk if ignored:* low — the proposal already has this mostly right; the only risk is deferring the index-free guarantee long enough for a third consumer to regress.
