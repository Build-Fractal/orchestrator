M014 closes the M015 bootstrapping loop — the project ripped out spec-kit at M015 cutover but had no replacement surface; M014 ships the native equivalent. Four phases, 24 tasks, ~727 minutes of dispatch time, 48+ verifiers across four phase suites, 119/119 cross-phase validation checks PASS.

**P01 (157m, 7 tasks, 14-gate suite)** shipped the load-bearing foundation: native `orchestrator:specify` create-path, `templates/spec-template.md` as Section Contract SSOT, `scripts/util/dual-write-runtime-md.sh` with SC-6a outside-markers byte-preservation invariant, `scripts/verify/spec-shape-lint.sh` shape linter, `tests/test-specify-shape.sh` byte-compat fixture, `spec-complexity-probe.sh` stub, `commands/specify.md` with deferred-body subcommand surface for `--amend` and `split`, `RUNTIME-ASSUMPTIONS.md` registry scaffold per D016.

**P02 (160m, 6 tasks, 9-gate suite)** extended dual-write to every enumerated `CLAUDE.md` write-site (`init-project.sh`, `reinit-handler.sh`, `consolidate-artifacts.sh`), shipped FR-13 drift detector in `check-docs.sh --check drift` mode with advisory severity model, migrated 7 legacy Recent Changes entries from heading-based section into the marker-bounded region via `scripts/migrate/m014-p02-migrate-recent-changes.sh`, wired `runtime_instruction_drift` section into `orchestrator:doctor`.

**P04 (240m, 7 tasks, 12-gate suite)** converted P01 stubs into full implementations — FR-5 complexity probe with five axes and OR-semantics plus `hardening_spec_exception` boolean override; US-3 three-way prompt wiring conversus adapter via `--strict` invocation; FR-7 `split` subcommand with LLM-assisted decomposition (CC-only v1); FR-14 three-case `--amend` body; `templates/conversus-presets/spec-pressure-test.yml` red-blue preset; calibration corpus pinned thresholds; `references/spec-management.md` SC-11 completion.

**P03 (170m, 5 tasks, 14-gate suite)** closed the original M014 mission — wiki Giscus + GitHub Issue/PR comments classify into uat-bug / decision-append / spec-amendment / ambiguous; two trivial classes auto-apply above threshold (uat-bug via M013/FR-10 UAT ingestion; decision-append via templated DECISIONS.md row append); spec-amendment ALWAYS queues for human sign-off (CON-5/SC-5 two-tier verification); ambiguous routes through conversus adapter under `--strict` with adapter-missing graceful-degradation to human triage bucket. FR-9 classifier shape pinned to regex/heuristic v1 baseline per D023 with explicit retune trigger (≥30 actioned comments OR ≥20 percent calibration divergence). D023 also retired the synthetic P99 dependency placeholder from the roadmap by replacing it with an explicit Preflight note covering the M012 wiki deploy (resolved 2026-04-23) and the SC-16 inbox-dogfood data capture (relaxed to best-available signal).

**Two D-row decisions captured during M014/P01 dogfood** on M020+M026 drove downstream evolution: D019 reshaped `orchestrator:specify` from scaffold-plus-optional-gate into a three-pass flow (scaffold → author → gate) intensity-scaled with a universal TODO pre-flight guard on the conversus gate adapter; D020 captured two friction points (`spec-metrics.sh` chunks-first scoping bug + scaffold-placeholder-token-in-backticks gate false-positive). D019's shell implementation in `scripts/specify/specify.sh` landed incrementally across M014/P04; the three-pass contract is the SSOT in `commands/specify.md`.

**Side-fixes bundled along the way**: `auto-loop.sh --step=V` parser scope fix (M026/P02 bbt-companion dogfood — was extracting informational backticks in Inputs/Constraints sections as commands); `read-roadmap.sh` Risk-field truncation (M026/P02 — multi-word Risk values like "Risk: low — prose" now parse cleanly); `auto-loop.sh` slug-bearing `T##-<slug>-PLAN.md` support (M014/P03/T01 dispatch — the one consumer that used literal-concat path rather than `T*-PLAN.md` glob).

**Conversus adapter** (`scripts/dispatch/adapters/tool/conversus.sh`) is byte-identical pre/post M014 per D007 reuse discipline — M014 shipped two new presets (`spec-pressure-test.yml` in P04, `classify-comment.yml` in P03) without a single line of adapter modification.

The bootstrapping loop closes: the next milestone's spec (M020 is the first candidate) will be authored via `orchestrator:specify` rather than hand-copied. SC-13 is the dogfood gate and is satisfied when exercised.
