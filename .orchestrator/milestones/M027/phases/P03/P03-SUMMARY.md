## Lessons carried forward to M027 milestone summary + future work

- **T04-subsumes-prechecks pattern** is now fully canonical (M027/P00, P01, P02, P03 — four occurrences). Plan-phase agents should always generate per-task scoped prechecks for tasks shipping structural changes; T04 deletes them once canonical phase-level verifiers ship.
