## Key decisions

- **T04 structurally distinct from T01–T03** — fuses phase-close + milestone-close into one atomic commit because splitting creates a fragile half-closed state and the `M030-VALIDATED` marker contract requires every `P0[0-7]-SUMMARY.md` present simultaneously.
- **One-shot evidence ledger** — committed once at T03, never regenerated dynamically. Operators re-run the battery for fresh evidence; the ledger is the milestone-close audit-trail snapshot.
- **Acceptance battery as straight-line SC delegator** — `run-acceptance-battery.sh` wraps every M030 SC verifier in literal sequence (no loops, no eval); same shape discipline as phase-suite aggregators.
- **Milestone-grain `unit_close` convention** — first-of-kind record on disk (no M028 precedent for milestone-grain unit_close). Convention: `granularity=milestone` + `phase_count=N` field; documented in T04-SUMMARY.md.
- **Plan-amendment-not-task-reopen** at T03 for 5 predicate divergences (line-count relaxation + key-link source inversion to a forward-direction reachable file with parenthetical reason).

## Patterns established (carry forward)

- **Structurally-distinct-T04 fusing phase + milestone close** — when the final phase IS the milestone-close gate, fuse the two ceremonies into one atomic commit. Splitting them creates a fragile two-commit close where the first commit leaves the milestone half-closed.
- **One-shot-evidence-ledger discipline** — committed snapshot at milestone close; never regenerated. Operators re-run the contract (the battery here) for fresh evidence; the ledger is the audit-trail.
- **Acceptance battery as straight-line SC delegator** over N verifier invocations — wraps every SC verifier in literal sequence and emits a canonical `BATTERY:` summary line; reusable shape for any future milestone with a multi-SC acceptance contract.
- **Idempotent acceptance-corpus synthesizer** with sha256-equality verifier — mirrors P05/T01 + P06/T01 lineage; first invocation creates corpora, second invocation produces byte-identical files.
- **9-gate straight-line phase-suite aggregator** extending P02–P06 lineage without shape change.
- **Evidence-ledger shape gate** combining frontmatter-key grep + body-section grep + word-boundary-aware SC-label grep (`grep -E '<label>([^A-Za-z0-9]|$)'` prevents SC-1 substring-matching SC-10/SC-11) + cited-verifier-path on-disk resolution via `grep -oE` + `sort -u` + `while read` loop.
- **Milestone-grain `unit_close` convention** — first-of-kind record introduced; future milestone closes can reuse the shape.
