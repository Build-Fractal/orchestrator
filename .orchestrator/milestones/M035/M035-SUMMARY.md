# M035 — packaging & distribution

M035 is the launch-readiness milestone for the orchestrator. Eight phases (P00, P01, P01.5, P02, P03, P04, P05, P06) shipped over ~1100 wall-clock minutes of spec-to-close work, covering pre-launch dev-ergonomics + the spec-kit-orchestrator → orchestrator namespace rename + three publishing channels (npm + Homebrew + curl-pipe-bash) + install-script integrity (sigstore keyless signing + rollback marker contract) + multi-source `orchestrator:update` dispatch + the milestone-grain acceptance battery that gates `validate-milestone.sh M035` and the milestone-close artifacts.
