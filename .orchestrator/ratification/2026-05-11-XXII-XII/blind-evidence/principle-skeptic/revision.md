### Position Summary

Of my eight original recommendations, I withdrew zero, modified two (recommendations 5 and 8), and maintained six. Both modifications are refinements rather than retreats: recommendation 5 gains a reader definition clause and a reader-exception escape hatch for dynamic reading patterns from the advocate's cross-review, strengthening the scope-narrowing without reopening unverified artifact classes to the principle's enforcement scope; recommendation 8 is absorbed into recommendation 2's Quality Gates reclassification because its substance — a defined triggering condition — is load-bearing for that reclassification and should travel with it rather than stand as a separately-tracked item.

The most significant change in my thinking is structural rather than substantive. The advocate's cross-review surfaced the PENDING/ACTIVE tier as a genuine mechanism for handling the feasibility-vs-sufficiency distinction at ratification time, which I had diagnosed as a problem but not resolved. My new recommendation 2 addresses this directly with the consequence clause addition. The underlying diagnosis — that stub verifiers cannot constitute mechanical enforcement — was correct in my original review; what I missed is that the correct response to that diagnosis is a structural tiered-status mechanism with a named consequence, not a flat ratification block that the advocate's cross-review reasonably characterizes as overly rigid. The consequence clause (automatic demotion to ADVISORY if the promotion deadline is missed) is what distinguishes this from repeating the M033 deadline-overrun pattern documented in CLAUDE.md.

My highest-priority surviving recommendations are 3 and 4 together — supply CONFORMANCE.md and resolve VIII's "configuration entry" ambiguity as hard prerequisites before any ratification verdict issues. The advocate's cross-review confirmed both as load-bearing preconditions, and the cross-reviews reached independent convergence on both through different reasoning paths. Without CONFORMANCE.md, criterion (i) is BINDING INCOMPLETE and no ratification verdict is procedurally valid. Without the PATCH amendment to VIII, Candidate B's entire distinctness argument rests on a textual reading that the cross-review confirmed is contestable by any future maintainer who reads VIII's enumeration literally. These two prerequisites are the conditions under which the synthesis arbiter's verdict is trustworthy; every other recommendation depends on them being satisfied first.
