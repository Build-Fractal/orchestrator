### Final Position Statement

**Non-Negotiables**

1. Operational term definitions must be added to Candidate A's Invariant 3 text at or before ratification — not in a follow-on amendment. (Surviving Rec 2.) Undefined operational terms in a principle's normative clause constitute a falsifiable-scope inclusion criterion failure, not merely an evidence gap warranting Provisional status. Conditional ratification is the correct mechanism for distinctness documentation gaps; it is not the correct mechanism for a principle text that cannot be mechanically falsified as written. The inclusion criteria test applies to the principle text at ratification time, and "works" is not a falsifiable term.

2. The Principle VIII PATCH must be on disk before Candidate B is ratified. (New Rec.) The scope boundary between VIII (file-system reachability) and Candidate B (config-knob liveness) is load-bearing for Candidate B's distinctness argument. If that boundary is recoverable only by cross-referencing CONFORMANCE.md's three-bucket table, then Principle XI (Single Source of Truth) is violated: the boundary has two authoritative locations, and a reader with only the constitution's plain text cannot determine governance scope. The PATCH text is narrow, agreed, and already drafted. There is no principled argument for deferring it.

3. SKILL.md-equivalent frontmatter scope must be removed from Candidate B's normative body before ratification. (Modified Rec 3.) A principle that declares a scope its linter cannot enforce misrepresents its mechanical verification coverage. CONFORMANCE.md's clause-mapping scaffold already labels this cell PENDING; the principle's normative body should not declare a scope that its own evidence table marks as undetermined. Scope-narrowing before ratification is always cheaper than scope-narrowing after the principle is in the constitution with enforcement expectations attached.

**Flexibility**

1. The timing of the Candidate A restructure and X sentence (Modified Rec 4, sub-steps (b)–(c)). I prefer both as ratification-time editorial changes since their content is agreed. I can accept their placement in the follow-on amendment if and only if the CONFORMANCE.md Provisional-cap condition is written to explicitly cover the restructure and the X sentence — not only the XI structural argument. The cap must be comprehensive: "the row cannot advance from Provisional until (a) the XI structural distinctness argument is authored, (b) the Principle X distinctness sentence is on record within the restructured Candidate A text, and (c) the restructured form leads with Invariant 3." If the cap covers all three, the timing of the commits is an implementation detail.

2. The bundled-vs.-sequenced framing for follow-on amendment work. (Dispute with skeptic's Modified Rec 2.) I am flexible on whether XI + X + operational definitions (if the synthesizer accepts the skeptic's bundling position on Dispute 1) must land in a single commit. What must be preserved is the Provisional-cap mechanism as the enforcement gate: status advancement requires all conditions to be satisfied simultaneously, regardless of whether they were authored in one commit or several. If the skeptic's single-commit requirement is adopted, it must be stated as an authoring discipline rather than a constitutional requirement — the Provisional cap is the operative enforcement; the commit discipline is operational guidance, not a constitutional clause.
