### Executive Summary

The proposal declares two inheritance relationships — conversus Tier 2 XXII (Distribution Surface Integrity) and Tier 2 XII (No Dead Infrastructure) — alongside five supporting changes including a Principle I clarification, a governance log, an operator-subtraction asymmetry reference, and a planning lens. From a coherence standpoint, the inheritance-not-authoring pattern is the right structural choice: it aligns with Principle XI (Single Source of Truth) and avoids the duplication risk the original Change 3 draft would have created. The XII/VIII scope-split is conceptually sound.

Two coherence defects demand resolution before this ratification proceeds. The first is procedural and acute: `.orchestrator/memory/constitution.md` at L175-185 already contains the amendment's text in Principle VIII's "Tier 2 alignment" note, attributed to "the XXII + XII inheritance amendment" — yet this document is the originating deliberation in the three-deliberation ratification sequence the proposal itself defines. State On Disk Is Truth (Principle VI, L125-138) means the constitution, as it currently sits on disk, claims a ratification event that has not occurred. The second is substantive: Change 5's proposed clarification to Principle I leaves the `Context_Efficiency` formula at L48 contradicting the amendment's own argument. Both are fixable; neither is a reason to reject the inheritance declarations themselves.

**Most important recommendation**: remove the "Tier 2 alignment" note from Principle VIII in the current constitution and hold it as a pending change, landing it only after the third deliberation closes and the Governance version bump is recorded.

### Alignment

- **Inheritance-not-authoring pattern** (proposal L29-34): The decision to declare inheritance of XXII rather than author a new orchestrator Principle XVI directly applies Principle XI (Single Source of Truth, constitution L217-230): one authoritative location (conversus XXII) rather than two parallel definitions that could drift. The same logic correctly governs the XII declaration at proposal L35-41.

- **CONFORMANCE.md as cross-suite bridge** (proposal L31, L39): Routing both inheritance declarations through `CONFORMANCE.md`'s Component-tier declarations table gives XI a single machine-readable join point between the build-fractal namespace and the orchestrator's component-tier namespace. This is precisely the "one authoritative location" XI demands for cross-project knowledge.

- **Evidence-on-disk before declaration** (proposal L37): `check-dead-infra.sh` and `tests/test-dead-infra-knobs.sh` exist with a passing baseline (0 dead / 41 leaves scanned) before the inheritance is declared. This satisfies Principle II (Evidence Before Claims, constitution L52-74) in spirit — the inherited principle's enforcement mechanism is operational, not aspirational.

- **Change 5 falsifiable scope** (proposal L144): The proposed clarification to Principle I includes a machine-testable falsifiability criterion ("a verifier could compare JSONL token totals on fixtures with-knowledge vs without-knowledge") that satisfies the inclusion criteria logic the proposal itself establishes. This is consistent with Principle II's requirement that verification be a mechanical gate, not an LLM compliance exercise (constitution L60-61).

- **XII/VIII scope boundary** (proposal L40-41, constitution L175-185): The acknowledgment that orchestrator Principle VIII governs file-system-level infrastructure while inherited Tier 2 XII governs config-knob and schema-variable surfaces is a correct separation. Both levels exist; VIII's "configuration entry" phrasing in constitution L163 is not the same scope as XII's "documented-consumer surfaces." The distinction is real, not cosmetic.

### Missed Opportunities

- **No version bump specified**: The Governance section requires "Explicit version bump following semantic versioning" as the first requirement for any amendment (constitution L339-342). The proposal neither specifies the resulting version number nor does the on-disk constitution's version line (L355: "Version: 2.1.0 | Ratified: 2026-03-18 | Last Amended: 2026-04-14") reflect a pending change. Change 5 is materially expanded guidance on an existing principle (MINOR per the Governance rubric at L342). The inheritance declarations add a new governance mechanism (also MINOR). The combined amendment produces 2.2.0. Not naming the target version leaves implementers without a deterministic artifact to commit. Impact: **high** — Governance L339 is a hard requirement, not optional prose.

- **Principle I formula contradiction**: Constitution L48 defines the optimization target as `Context_Efficiency = Relevant_Instructions / Total_Instructions_Inherited`. Change 5 argues that minimizing payload bytes (the current formula's numerator-denominator framing) is the wrong optimization target, and that "total task tokens" — including exploration tokens and re-dispatch costs — is what matters (proposal L136-143). If Change 5 lands, this formula no longer captures the stated intent. A future reader will see a formula that optimizes payload ratio and text below it that says payload ratio is the wrong thing to optimize. The formula should either be updated (`Total_Task_Tokens = Dispatch_Tokens + max(Knowledge_Injection, Exploration_Recovery)`) or explicitly annotated as measuring a proxy, not the true target. Impact: **high** — the contradiction is internal to the same principle.

- **Template propagation not enumerated**: Governance L345 requires "Consistency propagation across all dependent templates." The proposal's out-of-scope section (L179) explicitly declines to touch the Sync Impact Report header, but provides no list of templates requiring update for the Governance cross-reference (Change 1) or the Principle I clarification (Change 5). The 2.0.0 → 2.1.0 Sync Impact Report (constitution L21-24) names `templates/phase-plan.md` as the affected template. This amendment's Sync Impact Report comment block — which must be authored as part of the change — has no draft in the proposal. Impact: **medium** — the gap will surface during implementation when the author must decide what to update without prior guidance.

- **VIII/XII boundary invisible from constitution alone**: The Principle VIII "Tier 2 alignment" note (constitution L175-185) tells readers to consult `CONFORMANCE.md` for the scope boundary. But the Governance section (L334-353) contains no pointer to CONFORMANCE.md as an authoritative cross-reference for inheritance declarations. A future amendment author reading only the constitution will not know that inherited principles' scope boundaries live in CONFORMANCE.md. Adding a single sentence to Governance ("Component-tier inheritance declarations and their scope boundaries are maintained in `CONFORMANCE.md`") would make the system self-describing without adding bulk. Impact: **medium**.

- **Design-before-code inversion for XII not documented**: Principle III (constitution L76-92) requires the pipeline "brainstorm → plan → execute → review" with "No implementation without an approved design." The XII inheritance's enforcement scripts (`check-dead-infra.sh`, `tests/test-dead-infra-knobs.sh`) were shipped during the Tier C alignment sweep before the inheritance was declared or this ratification begun (proposal L37: "The capability is on disk; the inheritance declaration is not"). This is a Principle III inversion — the implementation preceded the design approval. The proposal does not acknowledge this as an exception, document why it is acceptable (e.g., the linter was written as evidence-gathering prior to a deliberation, which is itself a valid design-stage activity), or note it in the CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md backfill entries. Impact: **medium** — future authors will model this as acceptable practice without understanding the exception's limits.

- **CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md not self-referential at genesis**: The proposal specifies backfilling "the 4 most recent constitutional amendments (XIII-XV plus the most recent sync)" (proposal L103). But the present amendment — the event that *creates* the log — is not included as an open entry. The log's genesis entry should record its own creation event (Type: Amendment, Status: Open) so the document's first state demonstrates the format and closes the bootstrap circularity. This is a pattern conversus uses: the `CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md` it was adopted from (proposal L91) contains entries for its own creation. Impact: **low** — not a blocking gap, but the log will start in an incoherent state.

- **Per-change PASS/BLOCK criteria absent from ratification path**: The ratification path (proposal L47-49) defines outputs as "PASS / FLAG / BLOCK per principle" but does not enumerate what a BLOCK looks like for each of the five discrete changes. Change 5 (Principle I clarification) and the XII/XXII inheritance declarations have different risk profiles and different failure modes. A shared BLOCK definition means the three deliberations cannot produce calibrated per-change verdicts. This makes the deliberation results harder to act on when one change passes and another flags. Impact: **low** — the deliberation can still function; the gap produces ambiguous verdicts, not absent ones.

### Off-Base Assumptions

- **"Tier 2 XII and Change 5 are in tension"** (proposal charter): The charter asks whether "Change 5's clarification of Principle I is in tension with the XII inheritance's framing of 'fully defined, fully typed, fully dead' infrastructure." This tension does not exist. XII deletes dead config knobs from the supply side of the context pipeline; Change 5 corrects the optimization target on the consumption side (total task tokens vs payload bytes). They are orthogonal and mutually reinforcing: if XII removes dead knobs, the knowledge injection pipeline carries less waste, making Change 5's "minimum sufficient context via the cheapest pipeline" cheaper to satisfy. Treating this as a tension worth testing would consume deliberation cycles on a non-issue.

- **"The version line is outside scope"** (proposal L179): The out-of-scope section says "Touching the orchestrator's existing Sync Impact Report header" is excluded, explaining the log supplements rather than replaces the header. But the version line at constitution L355 is not the Sync Impact Report header — it is the constitution's canonical version identifier, distinct from the comment block at L1-29. Governance L339-342 explicitly requires a version bump as part of the amendment. The proposal appears to conflate "the existing Sync Impact Report comment block" (which it rightly leaves alone as audit trail) with "the version bump the Governance section mandates" (which is a required amendment output). These are separate artifacts. The version bump at L355 cannot be deferred as out-of-scope.

- **"No collision between Tier 2 XII and orchestrator XII"** (proposal L41): The claim that "Tier 1 / Tier 2 numerals operate in the build-fractal namespace; orchestrator's component-tier numerals operate in the constitution namespace" is technically accurate but misleads about the reader experience. Principle VIII's "Tier 2 alignment" note (constitution L175-176) writes "Tier 2 XII" inside the constitution document, where a reader's nearest referent for "XII" is constitution §XII (Hook Isolation, L232-247). The namespace claim is correct; the collision *for a constitution reader* is real. The note should qualify "conversus Tier 2 XII" rather than bare "Tier 2 XII" to prevent the lookup confusion.

### Actionable Recommendations

1. **Remove pre-amendment from constitution VIII** (Priority: P1)
   - **Current state**: Constitution L175-185 contains the "Tier 2 alignment" paragraph attributed to "the XXII + XII inheritance amendment." This text was written into the document before the originating deliberation completed.
   - **Proposed change**: Delete L175-185 from the current constitution.md. Place the full text in a separate `PENDING-VIII-AMENDMENT.md` file in the ratification directory. Land the VIII paragraph — and the version bump — only after the third deliberation closes with a PASS verdict.
   - **Rationale**: Principle VI (constitution L125-138): "If it is not on disk, it did not happen. No exceptions." The corollary is: if it is on disk attributed to an event, that event must have happened. Pre-writing an amendment into the authoritative document before ratification creates a false state that the constitution itself prohibits.
   - **Risk if ignored**: The second deliberation (self-consistency) and third deliberation (blind) are reviewing a constitution that already contains the change, defeating their purpose. The blind deliberation in particular is supposed to catch first-deliberation echo bias — it cannot do so if the amendment text is already embedded as settled truth in the source document.

2. **Specify the version bump target** (Priority: P1)
   - **Current state**: Constitution L355 reads "Version: 2.1.0 | Ratified: 2026-03-18 | Last Amended: 2026-04-14." The proposal does not name a target version, and the out-of-scope section's language about the Sync Impact Report header may have inadvertently framed the version line as excluded.
   - **Proposed change**: Add to the proposal's ratification path section: "Upon PASS verdict, the version line advances to 2.2.0. The Sync Impact Report comment block documents: MINOR amendment; adds Governance §Inheritance reference paragraph (Change 1), CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md (Change 2), Tier 2 XXII + XII inheritance declarations (Change 3 + new), Principle I expanded guidance (Change 5). Principles I, VIII unchanged by number."
   - **Rationale**: Governance L339-342: "Amendments require: 1. Explicit version bump following semantic versioning." This is the first requirement, not a courtesy. The semantic version is also the primary signal in `CHANGELOG.md` that an amendment was ratified.
   - **Risk if ignored**: The amendment lands without a discoverable version marker. Future authors cannot determine when the governance cross-reference or Principle I clarification became canonical from the version history alone.

3. **Update the Principle I optimization formula** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: Constitution L48 defines `Context_Efficiency = Relevant_Instructions / Total_Instructions_Inherited`. Change 5 (proposal L140-143) argues this formula captures the wrong metric — payload ratio rather than total task tokens including recovery costs.
   - **Proposed change**: Update the formula in the Change 5 body to either replace L48 (`Total_Task_Tokens_Minimized = Dispatch_Tokens + min(Knowledge_Injection_Cost, Exploration_Recovery_Cost)` with a note that the old ratio measured a proxy), or annotate the existing formula: "This ratio measures payload efficiency; the governing optimization target is total task tokens across the full dispatch cycle, including potential recovery." The formula in the proposal should match whichever form lands.
   - **Rationale**: Internal contradiction within the same principle: the formula at L48 optimizes what Change 5 explicitly calls the wrong thing (L136-138: "minimizing payload bytes... sometimes inversely related" to the right thing). Principle II (L60-61) requires mechanical verification; a formula that optimizes the wrong metric is not a mechanical gate.
   - **Risk if ignored**: The formula will be cited against Change 5's intent in future deliberations. The next author to implement a payload-reduction optimization will cite L48 as justification while violating the clarification.

4. **Add CONFORMANCE.md pointer to Governance section** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: The Governance section (constitution L334-353) contains no reference to CONFORMANCE.md. The inheritance declarations are routed through CONFORMANCE.md (proposal L31, L39), but this routing is only discoverable via the Principle VIII note (L181-183) or the proposal itself — not from the Governance section that governs amendments.
   - **Proposed change**: Add a sentence to Governance after L345: "Component-tier inheritance declarations and their scope boundaries are maintained in `CONFORMANCE.md`; amendments that declare inheritance must include a corresponding CONFORMANCE.md update."
   - **Rationale**: Principle XI (L217-221): "Every piece of orchestrator state, configuration, and knowledge MUST have exactly one authoritative location." The authoritative routing for inheritance lives in CONFORMANCE.md; the Governance section that governs amendments must point there or XI's single-source guarantee is incomplete for future amendment authors.
   - **Risk if ignored**: The next amendment that touches an inherited principle will author the change without knowing to update CONFORMANCE.md, producing undeclared inheritance with no scope boundary.

5. **Document XII design-before-code inversion as a bounded exception** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: Proposal L37 states "The capability is on disk; the inheritance declaration is not" without acknowledging the Principle III inversion this represents.
   - **Proposed change**: Add to the proposal's "Adds (new): Tier 2 XII" section: "Note (Principle III): the `check-dead-infra.sh` linter and test suite were implemented during the Tier C alignment sweep prior to this ratification. This inverts the brainstorm → plan → execute → review pipeline. The exception is narrow: the linter was written as discovery-phase evidence to establish that the inheritance was satisfiable before declaring it — analogous to writing a feasibility spike before committing to a design. The Tier C port must not be used as precedent for implementing enforcement tools before the inherited principle is ratified." Log this in the CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md backfill as the triggering incident for the exception.
   - **Rationale**: Principle III (L84-86): "No implementation without an approved design. The design gate is a HARD GATE." Undocumented exceptions to hard gates become uncontrolled precedent.
   - **Risk if ignored**: Future alignment sweeps will ship enforcement scripts before ratification under "this is how XII was done," eroding the Principle III gate systematically.

6. **Qualify "Tier 2 XII" as "conversus Tier 2 XII" in Principle VIII note** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: Constitution L175-176: "**Tier 2 alignment** (added 2026-05-11 by the XXII + XII inheritance amendment): the build-fractal namespace carries Tier 2 XII (No Dead Infrastructure)..." A reader's nearest referent for "XII" is the orchestrator's own Principle XII (Hook Isolation, L232).
   - **Proposed change**: Replace "Tier 2 XII" in L176 and L184 with "conversus Tier 2 XII" throughout the Principle VIII note, and wherever the bare "Tier 2 XII" form appears in CONFORMANCE.md rows added by this amendment.
   - **Rationale**: The proposal acknowledges the namespace distinction (proposal L41) but relies on readers knowing it without in-document disambiguation. The constitution is the document least likely to be read with the proposal's namespace note in context.
   - **Risk if ignored**: Low-frequency but high-confusion: readers encountering the VIII note will navigate to constitution §XII expecting infrastructure coverage semantics and find hook isolation semantics instead.

7. **Add genesis entry to CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md backfill** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: Proposal L103 specifies "backfill the 4 most recent constitutional amendments (XIII-XV plus the most recent sync)." The present amendment — the event that creates the log — is not included.
   - **Proposed change**: Prepend an open entry to the backfill list: `## 2026-05-11 — XXII + XII Inheritance Amendment, Type: Amendment, Status: Open`. Close it to Status: Closed upon third deliberation PASS, referencing the ratification directory artifacts.
   - **Rationale**: Principle VI (L138): "If it is not on disk, it did not happen." The log's own creation event is the most important thing that happened on the date the log was created. A log that does not record its own genesis has a gap at position zero that undermines its claim to completeness.
   - **Risk if ignored**: Low on its own. But CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md read six months later will have no record of what triggered the XXII + XII inheritance decision or what the three deliberations found — exactly the gap the log was created to prevent.

8. **Enumerate per-change PASS/BLOCK criteria** (Priority: P3)
   - **Current state**: Ratification path (proposal L47-49) specifies "PASS / FLAG / BLOCK per principle" but does not enumerate what BLOCK looks like for Change 5 (Principle I clarification) versus the XII/XXII inheritance declarations.
   - **Proposed change**: Add acceptance criteria per change: for the inheritance declarations, BLOCK = any existing principle is weakened or contradicted without a Complexity Tracking justification; for Change 5, BLOCK = the proposed clarification produces a falsifiability regression (i.e., the new text is less checkable than the old text); for Change 2, BLOCK = the log format is incompatible with conversus's CONSTITUTIONAL_CONVERSATIONS.md entry format.
   - **Rationale**: Principle II (L60-62): "Verification is a mechanical gate, not an LLM compliance exercise. Must-haves... MUST be checkable without human judgment." Deliberation verdicts without pre-stated criteria require human judgment to interpret.
   - **Risk if ignored**: When one deliberation returns FLAG on Change 5 and PASS on the inheritance declarations, the ratification path provides no rule for whether the overall verdict is PASS or FLAG. The ambiguity must be resolved ad hoc, which is the failure mode the three-deliberation pattern exists to prevent.
