### Final Position Statement

**Non-Negotiables**

1. The proposal must include a specific, unambiguous disposition for `packaging/install/*.sh` script coverage — silence is not acceptable. The coverage-completeness claim ("VIII + inherited Tier 2 XII discharge the orchestrator's full dead-infrastructure coverage") is the ratification's load-bearing assertion. A deliberation ratifying an incomplete coverage claim produces a false PASS that cannot be corrected post-ratification without a new amendment cycle. The cost of resolving this before synthesis is a single sentence; the cost of resolving it after PASS is an additional amendment invocation against a ratified constitution. Option (b) — declare installer scripts out of XII scope, governed by XXII — is the specified disposition; if the synthesizer selects option (a) instead, the synthesis must include a linter scope-filter specification or it introduces a new precision defect while closing the coverage gap.

2. Phase 2 scope-precision text must be framed as CONFORMANCE.md-authoritative, not as contingent on L175-185's ratification. The two-phase sequencing rule exists precisely to establish CONFORMANCE.md as the Phase 2 evidentiary foundation — "contingent on L175-185 landing" reintroduces a dependency on pending text that Phase 1 is designed to eliminate. The correct framing is: Phase 2 text is independently grounded in CONFORMANCE.md now; L175-185's eventual ratification adds a constitutional cross-reference to the same scope map. These framings produce different implementer behavior: the CONFORMANCE.md-authoritative framing allows Phase 2 work to proceed immediately after Phase 1 completes; the "contingent" framing delays it until after the third deliberation — a false constraint with no evidentiary basis.

**Flexibility**

1. The pairwise interaction paragraph (Rec 3) describing how conversus Tier 2 XII and XXII apply independently to dual-governed artifacts. I am flexible on the concrete example used and on the paragraph's exact placement in the proposal. What must be preserved is the substantive analysis: XII governs config-knob liveness, XXII governs distribution-surface correctness, they apply as non-overlapping sub-properties of dual-governed artifacts, and neither principle is load-bearing for the other when the artifact satisfies both simultaneously. The analysis must appear in the proposal — not only in the pairwise interaction paragraph of the deliberation record — so the blind deliberation receives it as proposal content, not as review meta-commentary.

2. The installed-artifact reachability question (Rec 4) form and placement. I hold that the question must appear visibly in the proposal (Dispute 2), but I am flexible on the form: it can appear as a sentence in the XXII inheritance note, as an item in the proposal's risk section, or as an entry in the ratification path's per-change criteria — any form that places the question in the proposal text before the self-consistency deliberation receives it. The non-negotiable element is visibility in the proposal; the flexible element is the section in which that visibility is established.
