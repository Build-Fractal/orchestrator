### Executive Summary

The proposal declares joint Tier 2 XXII (Distribution Surface Integrity) and XII (No Dead Infrastructure) inheritance in a single deliberation set, arguing the two share the same inheritance-not-authoring shape. XXII governs installer/packaging/distribution surfaces; XII governs config-knob/schema-variable/documented-consumer surfaces. This review finds that the primary domains are genuinely distinct — no head-on contradiction exists between the two sets of invariants — but the proposal leaves three concrete interactions unresolved: (1) config knobs inside `packaging/install/*.sh` installer scripts fall outside XII's declared four-directory scope (`templates/`, `scripts/`, `commands/`, `references/`) while also falling outside XXII's explicit invariants, which govern distribution-behavior correctness rather than internal variable hygiene, creating an ungoverned artifact class; (2) the proposal includes `references/` in XII's scope without acknowledging CONFORMANCE.md's Extended bucket, which explicitly routes reference-doc file-system reachability to Principle VIII rather than to inherited XII, producing a surface inconsistency between the proposal text and the three-bucket structure that the originating-deliberation P1 fixes already authored; and (3) the single-deliberation-set justification (L45) argues adoption feasibility via shape similarity but does not discharge the pairwise scope-separation question — specifically, it does not state which principle is load-bearing when a config knob governs a distribution surface.

The CONFORMANCE.md three-bucket structure (L70–79) does substantial clarification work on the VIII/XII scope boundary, but the proposal — which is the ratification target — never cites the three-bucket structure by name. A reviewer reading only the proposal cannot reconstruct the full scope map without cross-referencing CONFORMANCE.md independently. The three P1 recommendations below address the gaps that will predictably surface in the blind deliberation's criterion (i) check; the P2 and P3 items improve completeness but are not blocking.

The most important recommendation: the XII scope declaration (proposal L39) must explicitly state that `packaging/install/` scripts are excluded from XII's linter scope and must cite CONFORMANCE.md's Extended bucket for `references/` coverage, or the proposal will arrive at the blind deliberation with an undeclared governance gap and an inconsistency between the ratification target and its own supporting evidence document.

### Alignment

- **Primary scope separation is clean** (proposal L31, L39): XXII's declared scope is "installer / packaging / distribution surfaces" and XII's declared scope is "config knobs / infrastructure surfaces in `templates/`, `scripts/`, `commands/`, and `references/`." These name different artifact classes — XXII governs behavioral correctness at the distribution boundary; XII governs declared-but-dead infrastructure at the configuration surface. The two primary scan targets (`packaging/bundle/<runtime>/manifest.txt` for XXII; `templates/orchestrator-config-default.yml` for XII) are disjoint. [constitution.md, L163–174; CONFORMANCE.md, L76]

- **Evidence scripts are in distinct namespaces** (proposal L31, L37): XXII's evidence set is `scripts/verify/version-source-of-truth.sh`, `manifest-coverage.sh`, `installer-smoke.sh`; XII's evidence is `scripts/diagnostics/check-dead-infra.sh`. They scan different artifact classes — installer behavior vs. config-knob liveness — with no overlapping stated scan targets. The paths are separate enough that a future CI configuration can gate each independently. [CONFORMANCE.md, L65–66]

- **VIII/XII scope boundary is pre-authored** (constitution.md L175–185): The Tier 2 alignment note added to Principle VIII declares: "VIII governs file-system-level infrastructure reachability with `scripts/diagnostics/run-doctor.sh` as the canonical audit. Inherited Tier 2 XII governs config-knob / schema-variable / documented-consumer surfaces with `scripts/diagnostics/check-dead-infra.sh`." This is a necessary condition for XII and XXII to coexist without displacing VIII, and the constitution already carries it. [CONFORMANCE.md, L70–79 three-bucket structure]

- **Force-include and dead-infra detection do not share a scan target** (proposal L116–117, L37): XXII's force-include invariant asserts that every file shipped in a bundle appears explicitly in `packaging/bundle/<runtime>/manifest.txt`. XII's dead-infra assertion applies to config-knob leaves in config templates. A manifest file is not a config-knob surface; `manifest.txt` is not within `check-dead-infra.sh`'s scan scope. The two linters do not claim jurisdiction over the same file class. [CONFORMANCE.md, L76 satisfied-bucket scope]

- **Single-source versioning and config-knob detection operate at different granularities** (proposal L115, L37): XXII invariant 1 requires all installers to derive their version from one canonical source (`CHANGELOG.md` top-line heading per CONFORMANCE.md L65). XII detects config knobs declared but never consumed. A version-source string referenced by all three installers trivially satisfies XII's documented-consumer test; the two invariants do not interact on this artifact class. [CONFORMANCE.md, L65 canonical version source note]

### Missed Opportunities

- **`packaging/install/` scripts excluded from XII scope silently**: The proposal declares XII covers "`templates/`, `scripts/`, `commands/`, and `references/`" (L39), naming four directories. `packaging/install/install-claude-code.sh` and siblings (named explicitly at proposal L114) are in `packaging/install/`, a fifth directory absent from XII's scope. These installer scripts may contain dead-knob-shaped internal variables — unused env vars, stale path constants, dead feature flags. XXII governs their distribution-behavior correctness, not internal variable hygiene. VIII governs file-system reachability of the scripts as files, not liveness of variables within them. The result is an ungoverned class: dead config-knob-shaped variables inside installer scripts are caught by neither XII's linter nor XXII's evidence scripts. The proposal should either add `packaging/install/` to XII's scope and extend `check-dead-infra.sh` accordingly, or explicitly declare this class is out of XII's scope with a rationale (e.g., "installer scripts' internal variable hygiene is governed by code review and XXII end-to-end install testing"). Estimate impact: **high** — three installer scripts are the primary XXII surface and their exclusion from XII is silent rather than deliberate. [proposal L39, L114; CONFORMANCE.md L65]

- **Proposal's `references/` inclusion ambiguous against CONFORMANCE.md Extended bucket**: The proposal says XII covers "`references/`" (L39) without qualification. CONFORMANCE.md L78 states: "file-system-level reachability of helper scripts and reference docs falls under orchestrator Principle VIII (`run-doctor.sh`), not under inherited Tier 2 XII. The Extended bucket records this scope decision so future auditors don't mis-attribute the coverage." These are reconcilable — XII governs config-knob-shaped content in reference docs; VIII governs whether reference doc files are reachable — but the proposal's plain language does not restrict XII's coverage of `references/` to config-knob content. A reader of the proposal alone can conclude XII governs all dead infrastructure in `references/`, contradicting the Extended bucket. The CONFORMANCE.md did the clarification work; the proposal, as ratification target, must carry it too. Estimate impact: **medium** — the three-bucket structure exists precisely because this ambiguity surfaced in the originating deliberation P1 fixes; leaving the proposal inconsistent with its resolution weakens the ratification artifact. [proposal L39; CONFORMANCE.md L78; constitution.md L175–185]

- **No priority rule for config knobs inside XXII evidence scripts**: `scripts/verify/version-source-of-truth.sh`, `scripts/verify/manifest-coverage.sh`, and `scripts/verify/installer-smoke.sh` reside in `scripts/` (XII's declared scope) and are also XXII evidence scripts (they discharge XXII's mechanical verification requirement). If `check-dead-infra.sh` flags a dead knob inside one of these three scripts, which principle's remediation requirement governs — XII's (remove the dead knob) or XXII's (ensure installer behavior is correctly verified)? They do not contradict, but the proposal offers no governance attribution. Without an explicit statement, future compliance checks on this artifact class have no authoritative source. Estimate impact: **low** — the remediation action would be identical under either principle; but the attribution should be named in a constitutional context. [proposal L31, L39; CONFORMANCE.md L65–66]

- **"Correctly-manifested dead file" edge case not addressed**: XXII's force-include invariant (L116–117) ensures every shipped file appears in the manifest. It does not require manifested files to be read from the installed location by any orchestrator code path. VIII requires every file, script, template, and configuration entry to be reachable from a live code path (constitution.md L163–164), but `run-doctor.sh`'s scan scope is described as the repository, not the installed artifact directory. If a reference doc ships in the bundle (satisfying XXII force-include), is installed, but is never referenced by any command or script, which principle flags this? XII's linter scans `templates/orchestrator-config-default.yml`, not installed artifacts. XXII's linters check manifest completeness, not downstream usage. Whether VIII's `run-doctor.sh` covers installed artifact directories is unstated in both documents. This edge case may be ungoverned. Estimate impact: **medium** — likely rare in practice (reference docs are typically cited from commands), but the proposal should either confirm `run-doctor.sh` covers installed-artifact reachability or acknowledge this as a known gap. [proposal L116–117; constitution.md L163–174; CONFORMANCE.md L76–79]

- **Single deliberation set justification does not discharge scope-separation analysis**: The proposal justifies one deliberation set (L45) by citing "same inheritance-not-authoring shape, same Tier 2 source, same evidence-on-disk posture." These arguments address the adoption question (is inheritance the right shape to use?) but not the scope-separation question (how do XXII and XII interact when both apply to the same artifact?). Specifically: if a config knob in `templates/orchestrator-config-default.yml` controls which runtime adapters are included in a bundle distribution run, is it a XII surface (config-knob liveness) or a XXII surface (distribution behavior correctness) or both independently? The single-deliberation justification does not answer this. Batching the adoption question is correct and efficient; it does not substitute for an explicit interaction analysis, which should appear in the proposal text rather than being deferred to deliberators. Estimate impact: **medium** — absent this analysis in the proposal, the self-consistency and blind deliberation passes will derive it independently and may reach different conclusions. [proposal L45; constitution.md L175–185; CONFORMANCE.md L72]

### Off-Base Assumptions

- **"Same evidence-on-disk posture" implies no interaction analysis is needed** (L45): The proposal uses "same evidence-on-disk posture" as a partial justification for a single deliberation set. "Evidence on disk" is a criterion for adoption feasibility (are the linter and verification scripts committed?), not for scope-separation completeness (do the two principles interact on any artifact class?). The proposal conflates the two questions. The correct understanding: same evidence posture is sufficient justification for batching the adoption question; it does not discharge the pairwise interaction analysis, which is a logically separate question that must be answered in the proposal text or the self-consistency deliberation will raise it as a FLAG.

- **`references/` inclusion in XII is self-explanatory** (L39): The proposal lists `references/` as a XII-governed directory without elaboration, implicitly assuming readers understand XII applies only to config-knob-shaped content in reference docs, not to their file-system reachability or to their bundled distribution. This assumption is incorrect: CONFORMANCE.md required an explicit Extended bucket precisely because this was not self-evident during the originating deliberation. A proposal that assumes readers will cross-reference CONFORMANCE.md to reconstruct the correct scope interpretation violates the spirit of Principle IV (Plans Assume Zero Context) — the scope must be stated in the ratification target itself. [constitution.md, L94–107 (Principle IV); CONFORMANCE.md L78]

### Actionable Recommendations

1. **Declare `packaging/install/` scope disposition explicitly** (Priority: P1)
   - **Current state**: Proposal L39 names four directories for XII scope: "`templates/`, `scripts/`, `commands/`, and `references/`." `packaging/install/` is absent without comment.
   - **Proposed change**: Add one sentence to the XII inheritance shape paragraph (L39): "Config-knob-shaped variables inside `packaging/install/*.sh` installer scripts are outside XII's linter scope; their hygiene is governed by XXII's end-to-end install testing invariant and standard code review, not by `check-dead-infra.sh`." Alternatively, if the intent is that XII does cover `packaging/install/`, add `packaging/install/` to the directory list and extend `check-dead-infra.sh`'s scan targets explicitly.
   - **Rationale**: Without this, there is no authoritative basis for deciding whether dead env vars in installer scripts constitute a XII violation. The gap is currently silent. [proposal L39, L114; CONFORMANCE.md L65]
   - **Risk if ignored**: Future compliance auditors will encounter dead variables in installer scripts, find no governing principle, and either suppress the finding (governance gap persists) or mis-attribute it to XXII (incorrect attribution), neither of which is acceptable in a constitutional context.

2. **Qualify `references/` inclusion in XII scope with an Extended-bucket callout** (Priority: P1)
   - **Current state**: Proposal L39 says XII covers "`references/`" without any qualification distinguishing config-knob content from file-system reachability.
   - **Proposed change**: Append to the XII scope statement (L39): "For `references/`: XII governs config-knob-shaped content within reference docs. File-system-level reachability of reference doc files is governed by Principle VIII per the Extended bucket in CONFORMANCE.md § Tier 2 XII — Three-bucket structure."
   - **Rationale**: CONFORMANCE.md L78 already draws this boundary correctly; the proposal — which is the ratification target — must be self-consistent with the document that resolved its own originating-deliberation disputes. A ratification artifact that is inconsistent with its supporting evidence document is a documentation defect. [proposal L39; CONFORMANCE.md L78; constitution.md L175–185]
   - **Risk if ignored**: The blind deliberation re-presents the proposal without provenance. A deliberator encountering "`references/` in XII scope" with no Extended-bucket callout has no basis for the correct interpretation and will likely FLAG the ambiguity, requiring a proposal revision that adds this text.

3. **Add a pairwise interaction paragraph for the "config knob governs a distribution surface" case** (Priority: P1)
   - **Current state**: XXII and XII are declared independently (L31, L39). No statement addresses the artifact class where both apply simultaneously.
   - **Proposed change**: Add a paragraph between the XII and XXII inheritance declarations: "When a config knob within XII's governed scope controls a distribution surface — for example, a `distribution.bundle_runtime:` entry in `templates/orchestrator-config-default.yml` that determines which runtime adapters are bundled — XII and XXII apply independently to distinct sub-properties: XII governs whether the config knob has a documented consumer (liveness); XXII governs whether the distribution behavior the knob controls satisfies the three distribution-surface invariants (correctness). No priority rule is required because the two principles govern non-overlapping sub-properties of the same artifact."
   - **Rationale**: This is the minimal analysis the single-deliberation-set justification omits. Without it, the scope-separation question is answered by the deliberation rather than the proposal, which is the wrong ordering. [proposal L45; constitution.md L175–185; CONFORMANCE.md L72]
   - **Risk if ignored**: The self-consistency deliberation will encounter a proposal that declares two inheritance scopes with a non-trivial intersection and no interaction rule, and will require a resolution before returning PASS — effectively making this a required item for ratification anyway.

4. **Address the "correctly-manifested dead file" edge case** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: XXII's force-include invariant (L116–117) governs manifest completeness. Neither the proposal nor CONFORMANCE.md specifies whether `run-doctor.sh` scans installed artifact directories or only the repository source tree.
   - **Proposed change**: Add one sentence to the XXII inheritance note: "XXII's force-include discipline is satisfied when the manifest completely covers shipped files. Whether installed artifacts are actively referenced from the installed location is a Principle VIII concern; `run-doctor.sh`'s scan scope should be extended to include the target installation directory if this coverage is required."
   - **Rationale**: Identifies the edge case explicitly rather than leaving it to the Extended bucket's catch-all. [proposal L116–117; constitution.md L163–174; CONFORMANCE.md L76–79]
   - **Risk if ignored**: A manifested reference doc that is never read from the install location satisfies XXII and escapes both XII and the current VIII scan, shipping in every bundle with no removal trigger.

5. **Separate adoption-feasibility argument from scope-separation argument in L45** (Priority: P2)
   - **Current state**: L45 uses "same inheritance-not-authoring shape, same Tier 2 source, same evidence-on-disk posture" to justify both that a single deliberation set is appropriate AND (implicitly) that no further interaction analysis is needed.
   - **Proposed change**: Add a sentence to L45: "The single deliberation set covers the adoption question: are both inheritance declarations justified? The scope-separation question — how do XXII and XII interact on dual-governed artifacts — is answered in the pairwise interaction paragraph above and is not delegated to the deliberation."
   - **Rationale**: Deliberators should not be responsible for deriving scope-separation analysis that the proposal authors are better positioned to specify. [proposal L45]
   - **Risk if ignored**: The blind deliberation may BLOCK on absent scope-separation analysis, requiring a revision that adds items 3, 1, and 2 above before it can advance.

6. **Name governance attribution for config knobs inside XXII evidence scripts** (Priority: P3)
   - **Current state**: `scripts/verify/*.sh` XXII evidence scripts are in XII's declared scope (`scripts/`) but their governance relationship is unstated.
   - **Proposed change**: Add a parenthetical in the XXII evidence-scripts note (near L31): "(These scripts reside in `scripts/` and are therefore XII-scoped for their internal config-knob hygiene; XXII governs their behavioral correctness as distribution-verification evidence. Both must be satisfied; no conflict.)"
   - **Rationale**: Explicit attribution is better than implicit in a constitutional context. A compliance auditor encountering a dead knob in `manifest-coverage.sh` needs a governing principle. [proposal L31, L39; CONFORMANCE.md L65–66]
   - **Risk if ignored**: Low — the remediation would be identical under either principle — but the absence of attribution is a documentation gap in a ratification artifact.

7. **Cite CONFORMANCE.md's three-bucket structure by section heading in the proposal** (Priority: P3)
   - **Current state**: The proposal references CONFORMANCE.md at L31 and implicitly at L39 but does not name the three-bucket structure or the Extended bucket.
   - **Proposed change**: Add a sentence to the XII inheritance shape description (end of L39 paragraph): "The authoritative scope map for XII enforcement decisions is in CONFORMANCE.md § Tier 2 XII — Three-bucket structure (Satisfied / Provisional / Extended); that section governs where coverage gaps are tracked and which surfaces fall under VIII versus inherited XII."
   - **Rationale**: The three-bucket structure does the most important scope-clarification work in the supporting documentation; it should be directly reachable from the proposal by section reference, not only by navigating CONFORMANCE.md independently. [CONFORMANCE.md L70–79]
   - **Risk if ignored**: Self-consistency and blind deliberators will need to navigate from proposal → CONFORMANCE.md → three-bucket section to verify scope coverage; a direct cite reduces the chance that deliberators work from an incomplete picture.
