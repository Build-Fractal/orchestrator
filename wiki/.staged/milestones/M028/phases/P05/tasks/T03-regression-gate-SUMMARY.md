T03 ships the M028 close-out regression gate (scripts/verify/m028/p05-regression-gate.sh) -- a single CI-runnable artifact that sequences four sub-gates (install-roundtrip, 27-entry corpus replay, per-finding run-all, downstream fixture replay) in stable dependency-aligned order and emits a consolidated 'M028 close-out: 4/4 sub-gates clean' verdict; plus two thin sub-gate clean verifiers (p05-corpus-replay-clean.sh, p05-run-all-clean.sh) wrapping their respective harnesses with grep-based output-shape assertions, mirroring T02's p05-downstream-fixture-clean.sh shape. The architecture is two-layer by design: the regression gate is the consolidated close-out artifact (one verdict over four sub-gates, useful for CI and operator-facing close-out evidence with non-short-circuit semantics); the per-sub-gate clean verifiers are the granular Truth-Check leaves used by check-must-haves.sh to surface per-Truth pass/fail in phase verification output. Both layers consume the same underlying harnesses; they are NOT redundant -- the clean verifiers are the granular surface for phase Truth-Checks while the regression gate is the rolled-up close-out surface. All three deliverables are AD-19 single-script-file flat shapes, bash 3.2 + POSIX-sh-safe, no jq dependency. The regression gate's run_sub_gate helper function uses AD-19 helper-function carve-out (documented at top-of-file) to permit the per-sub-gate body without violating the inline-shape classifier.
